Nancye D'Arienzo is a real estate agent with Tucker Realty, serving buyers and sellers with a hands-on, client-first approach. Every transaction is handled with care, professionalism, and dedication, from the very first conversation about your goals through the final signature at closing.

frequently asked questions

have any question?

  • How do I get started selling my home?
    The best first step is a conversation about your goals, timeline, and the property itself. From there, Nancye can help you prepare the home for market, set a pricing strategy, and put together a plan to attract the right buyers.
  • What areas do you serve?
    Nancye D'Arienzo works with buyers and sellers throughout the Mount Vernon, WA area and the surrounding communities. Reach out to discuss your specific location and needs.
  • How long does the closing process typically take?
    Once a home is under contract, closing typically takes around 30-45 days, depending on financing, inspections, and other contingencies. Nancye will keep you informed and guide you through each step along the way.
  • Do you work with buyers as well as sellers?
    Yes. Nancye represents both buyers and sellers, helping buyers navigate the search and offer process and helping sellers prepare, market, and negotiate the sale of their home.
