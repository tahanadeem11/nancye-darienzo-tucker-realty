OUR SELLING PROCESS

  • Prepare
  • Market
  • Negotiate
  • Close
Every successful sale starts with preparation. Nancye works with you to get your home ready for market — from recommending improvements to staging and setting the right pricing strategy so your property makes a strong first impression.
Your home is presented to the right buyers through thoughtful marketing — professional photography, online exposure, and targeted outreach designed to generate genuine interest and strong offers.
When offers come in, skilled negotiation protects your interests. Nancye reviews every offer with you and negotiates terms and price with your goals in mind, every step of the way.
From inspections to paperwork, Nancye guides you through every detail of closing — providing clear communication and support until the sale is complete.
